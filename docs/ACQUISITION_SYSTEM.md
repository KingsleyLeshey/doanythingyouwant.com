# Acquisition System

## North-star commercial metric
Do not optimize for traffic or requests alone.

Primary economic question:

> **What does it cost to acquire a paid, successfully fulfilled customer objective, and what contribution does that customer generate over time?**

## Funnel events
Record at minimum:
1. `landing_view`
2. `intent_started`
3. `intent_submitted`
4. `qualification_completed`
5. `offer_shown`
6. `checkout_started`
7. `payment_succeeded`
8. `job_started`
9. `job_verified`
10. `job_delivered`
11. `customer_accepted`
12. `rework_requested`
13. `refund_issued`
14. `repeat_purchase`
15. `referral_created`

Every event should preserve first-touch and current-touch acquisition attribution when known.

## Channel order
### 1. Founder/direct distribution
Manually target businesses matching the V0 ICP. The goal is learning speed, not scale.

### 2. High-intent organic
Begin with a very small number of genuinely useful pages built around real commercial intent. Do not create thousands of pages before evidence exists.

### 3. Free Outcome Plan
Let a prospect describe what they want and receive a useful scoped plan. Capture consented follow-up and treat the request as structured demand data.

### 4. Referrals
After a verified successful job, ask for referral/introduction and track it as a distinct acquisition source.

### 5. Partners
Test consultants/agencies/software providers that encounter customer needs they do not fulfill.

### 6. Paid acquisition
Only after conversion and unit economics are measurable. Paid traffic is an allocator of proven economics, not a substitute for a weak offer.

## Acquisition flywheel

```text
real demand
→ paid job
→ verified result
→ proof + operational learning
→ legitimate case study/tool/answer/template
→ search/AEO/community/referral discovery
→ more qualified demand
```

Customer permission and truthfulness govern what can become public proof.

## Content rules
Publish only content that is useful independently of the sale. Prioritize:
- actual customer questions;
- verified outcomes/case studies with permission;
- original benchmarks/data;
- tools and calculators;
- reusable templates;
- commercial comparison/decision content where genuinely useful.

No fabricated case studies, fake testimonials, invented benchmarks, or thin AI page farms.

## Economics
For each paid objective track:
- acquisition channel/campaign;
- attributable acquisition spend;
- offer/version/price;
- revenue collected/refunded;
- AI/model cost;
- browser/search/data/API cost;
- infrastructure allocation where material;
- human intervention cost/time;
- rework cost;
- direct contribution;
- repeat/upsell/referral revenue.

## V0 experiment cadence
Do not vary ten things at once. Prefer one controlled commercial change per experiment: ICP, promise, price, proof, landing-page message, outreach channel, or delivery shape.

Keep experiment state and result in durable repository/data records so agents do not repeat failed tests from memory loss.

## Expansion rule
Unsupported requests are not noise. Record their category, urgency, budget signal, acquisition source and whether the customer would accept a scoped follow-up.

A second fulfillment wedge should be chosen from observed demand + economics, not founder excitement.
