# Product Vision

## North-star experience
A customer should experience:

> I have a business problem.  
> I told Do Anything You Want what I need.  
> They understood it.  
> They proposed a credible outcome and price.  
> I approved what required approval.  
> They did the work.  
> They proved what was done.  
> I received the result.

The customer must not need to understand agent frameworks, models, automation tools, contractors, APIs, or vendors.

## One front door
The primary interface is:

**What do you want done?**

Natural-language intent is converted into a structured `Objective` containing at minimum:
- desired outcome;
- customer/business context;
- constraints;
- available resources;
- permissions;
- budget range;
- deadline;
- success/acceptance criteria;
- missing information;
- proposed plan;
- price/offer when commercially ready.

## Product layers
1. **Intent layer** — understands what the customer actually wants.
2. **Commercial layer** — qualifies, scopes, prices, takes payment, and records acquisition economics.
3. **Execution layer** — decomposes objectives into bounded jobs and routes work to replaceable workers/tools/providers.
4. **Permission layer** — prevents consequential actions without required authority.
5. **Evidence layer** — captures what was actually done and the source/proof for material claims.
6. **Verification layer** — independently determines whether acceptance criteria were satisfied.
7. **Result layer** — delivers artifacts/outcomes and captures customer feedback.
8. **Knowledge layer** — converts completed work into reusable playbooks and structured operating knowledge.
9. **Acquisition layer** — turns legitimate proof and customer questions into discoverability, referrals, tools, and future demand.

## Broad front door, narrow fulfillment
The front door may accept almost any legitimate business request, but the system must be honest about unsupported work. V0 only productizes work that can be fulfilled and verified economically.

The first productized wedge is the Acquisition Sprint. Other requests become structured demand observations until a fulfillment path is deliberately activated.

## Long-term destination
As evidence accumulates, the system may route different objectives to AI agents, deterministic software, browser execution, APIs, specialists, suppliers, or mixed workflows. The exact provider is an implementation detail.

The durable company asset should become the combination of:
- demand and acquisition channels;
- intent/outcome graph;
- conversion and pricing evidence;
- customer relationships;
- successful verified playbooks;
- proprietary operational evidence;
- distribution assets;
- increasingly efficient fulfillment.

## Anti-goals
Do not optimize the company for:
- maximum agent count;
- maximum autonomy;
- maximum infrastructure complexity;
- a giant marketplace of visible providers;
- thousands of thin AI-generated landing pages;
- vanity engagement;
- speculative 2036 architecture.

Optimize for profitable customer outcomes and compounding acquisition.
