# Founding Record

## Company
**Do Anything You Want**  
Primary domain: `doanythingyouwant.com`

## Promise
**Tell us what you want done. We make it happen.**

## Company thesis
The company is not an AI wrapper, freelancer marketplace, or generic agency. It is an **outcome-execution company** with one customer relationship and a replaceable fulfillment layer.

The durable strategic belief is:

> **THE BUSINESS IS ONE. ACQUISITION IS THE MOAT.**

AI models, agents, contractors, APIs, suppliers, and frameworks are fulfillment inputs. They should be replaceable. The company should own the demand relationship, accumulated intent/outcome data, conversion knowledge, successful playbooks, proof, and repeat customer relationship.

## What must be proven
The business deserves continued investment only if evidence supports all of these:
1. strangers can discover and understand the offer;
2. some will pay before results are fabricated or promised beyond evidence;
3. the company can fulfill the purchased outcome credibly;
4. direct fulfillment cost is materially below revenue;
5. the same class of work becomes more repeatable and automated;
6. completed work creates proof, knowledge, referrals, content, or other acquisition assets;
7. repeat or adjacent demand lowers effective CAC over time.

## First economic milestone
**Receive the first dollar from a stranger for a real outcome.**

The first version is not judged by number of agents, lines of code, pages indexed, dashboards, or model sophistication.

## Initial wedge
The universal intake remains broad, but the first deliberate fulfillment wedge is **B2B customer acquisition / qualified prospecting**.

Reason:
- businesses already pay meaningful amounts for pipeline creation;
- output can be defined and independently verified;
- the work can begin research-first without immediately sending communications;
- repeatability and recurring demand are plausible;
- the company can dogfood the same acquisition capability on itself;
- every delivered project teaches the core moat.

## What is explicitly not proven yet
- product-market fit;
- profitable paid acquisition;
- autonomous end-to-end execution;
- recurring revenue;
- ability to fulfill arbitrary customer requests;
- any customer result, case study, testimonial, or revenue claim.

No future agent may present those as facts until repository evidence exists.
