# Roadmap

This roadmap is dependency-ordered. The current operational cursor lives in `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`.

## D0 — Foundation
**Goal:** create durable project memory and a safe multi-agent operating model.

Exit:
- agent contract;
- founding record;
- product vision;
- V0 offer;
- architecture;
- acquisition instrumentation;
- quality gates;
- CI skeleton;
- staging plan.

## D1 — Universal intake
**Goal:** make `What do you want done?` real.

Build:
- conversion-focused homepage;
- anonymous request capture;
- Turnstile/rate limiting;
- session/customer identity foundation;
- structured `Intent` record;
- acquisition-source/event capture;
- admin view of raw demand.

Exit: a stranger can land, describe a legitimate business outcome, and produce a durable intent record with source attribution.

## D2 — Scope and qualification
**Goal:** translate free-form intent into an executable objective.

Build:
- onboarding conversation;
- objective schema;
- missing-information loop;
- permission classification;
- budget/deadline/success criteria;
- deterministic eligibility rules;
- planner adapter;
- unsupported-request handling.

Exit: V0-compatible requests produce a reviewable scope; unsupported requests are recorded without fake fulfillment promises.

## D3 — Commercial offer + payment
**Goal:** allow an unknown customer to purchase a defined Acquisition Sprint.

Build:
- offer/pricing record;
- acceptance criteria;
- checkout adapter;
- payment state machine;
- receipt/audit trail;
- refund/rework state foundation.

Owner gate: live payment-provider credentials/activation.

Exit: staging can exercise the entire purchase state machine; production can accept real payment only after Owner activation.

## D4 — Acquisition Sprint fulfillment
**Goal:** reliably execute the first paid outcome.

Workflow:
1. normalize ICP and exclusions;
2. research candidate companies;
3. verify company fit and evidence;
4. identify relevant buyer roles/public business contact routes where permitted;
5. score and rank;
6. generate campaign angles/message hypotheses;
7. verify deliverable against acceptance criteria;
8. create customer artifact;
9. record cost/time/evidence.

No autonomous external outreach in this phase.

Exit: a paid staging job can produce a verified, evidence-backed deliverable without manual database manipulation.

## D5 — Delivery, feedback, knowledge
**Goal:** complete the customer loop.

Build:
- project/status page;
- artifact delivery;
- customer accept/rework feedback;
- quality/rework ledger;
- structured job learning;
- playbook capture;
- permissioned case-study candidate record.

Exit: completed jobs leave durable commercial and operational evidence.

## D6 — Staging launch readiness
**Goal:** prove the complete pre-production journey.

Required:
- exact-SHA CI;
- D1 migration/recovery evidence;
- R2 artifact evidence;
- deterministic fixture/smoke journey;
- security headers and abuse controls;
- payment sandbox journey;
- cost ledger assertions;
- funnel telemetry assertions;
- rollback procedure.

Exit: GREEN staging receipt for exact reviewed SHA.

## D7 — First-dollar launch
**Goal:** make a stranger pay.

Owner actions:
- production/DNS authorization;
- legal/business/payment setup;
- required secrets;
- final public claims review.

Acquisition experiments:
- founder-led/direct outreach;
- high-intent organic landing page;
- free Outcome Plan lead magnet;
- targeted community/partner distribution;
- referral incentive after successful delivery.

Milestone: **$1 from a stranger**.

## D8 — Offer validation
**Goal:** prove more than a lucky sale.

Target evidence:
- at least 5 paid V0 jobs;
- at least 3 unrelated customers;
- positive aggregate direct contribution;
- documented conversion funnel;
- documented fulfillment/rework rate;
- no fabricated proof.

Decision: improve, reposition, narrow further, or kill.

## D9 — Repeatability + recurring acquisition
**Goal:** earn the right to automate and sell recurring execution.

Build only after evidence:
- reusable vertical/ICP playbooks;
- managed campaign option;
- approved external communication workflows;
- deliverability/compliance controls;
- recurring billing;
- customer acquisition dashboard;
- outcome/meeting/reply evidence model.

## D10 — Acquisition engine compounding
**Goal:** turn work into distribution.

Add:
- evidence-backed case studies;
- original benchmark/data pages;
- useful tools/templates;
- programmatic pages only where unique structured data justifies them;
- AEO/AI-search outputs;
- referral/partner loops;
- CAC allocation by request class.

## D11 — Second fulfillment wedge
Only after V0 is economically proven, compare accumulated unsupported requests and economics. Likely candidates: AI automation, market research, MVP builds, or another category revealed by demand.

The company remains one. New capability is added behind the same front door.
