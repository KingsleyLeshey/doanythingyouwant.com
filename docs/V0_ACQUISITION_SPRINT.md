# V0 — Acquisition Sprint

## Purpose
V0 exists to test whether a stranger will pay Do Anything You Want for a real, bounded business outcome that can be fulfilled, verified, and repeated.

The broad brand promise remains intact, but only this outcome is productized initially.

## Target customer
Early target:
- B2B founder/operator or small company;
- already has a sellable offer;
- typical customer value high enough that one good opportunity matters;
- can describe target geography/industry/problem;
- needs pipeline or a clearer prospecting system.

Avoid customers whose offer, legality, target market, or success criteria cannot be understood well enough to qualify prospects responsibly.

## Customer-facing promise
**We build you a verified, campaign-ready prospecting pack for the customers you actually want.**

V0 is not a promise of closed revenue, booked meetings, or outbound sending.

## Founding price hypothesis
**$499 fixed** for the first standard Acquisition Sprint.

This is an experiment, not a permanent price. Store price/version in the offer record so conversion and margin can be compared later.

## Standard deliverable
A standard sprint should include:
1. written ICP/qualification definition approved from the customer's request;
2. 50 target companies that meet the agreed criteria;
3. relevant buyer-role guidance and public business contact routes where legitimately available;
4. source/evidence links for material qualification claims;
5. fit score and concise reason for inclusion;
6. exclusions/uncertainties clearly marked;
7. three campaign angles/message hypotheses tailored to the ICP;
8. delivery as CSV plus readable summary;
9. evidence-backed verification receipt.

Exact quantities may be adjusted before purchase if the niche or evidence burden requires it. The final offer record controls.

## Acceptance criteria
A prospect counts only when the agreed required fields and evidence are present and the verifier can reproduce the fit judgment from cited evidence.

No record should be represented as `verified` merely because an LLM generated or inferred it.

The deliverable passes when:
- required count is satisfied or an explicitly documented evidence-limited exception is accepted;
- duplicate threshold is zero after normalization;
- required evidence exists;
- exclusion rules were applied;
- verifier returns `PASS`;
- artifact is readable and exportable;
- direct cost/time ledger is complete.

## Workflow
`INTENT → QUALIFY → SCOPE → OFFER → PAYMENT → RESEARCH → VERIFY → DELIVER → FEEDBACK → KNOWLEDGE`

### Research
- discover candidate companies from permitted public/business sources;
- capture source URL and observed-at time;
- extract only relevant business facts;
- normalize company identity;
- score against explicit ICP rules.

### Verification
Independent verifier checks:
- identity/duplicate collisions;
- evidence availability;
- qualification logic;
- required fields;
- suspicious hallucinated data;
- artifact counts/totals.

Failure triggers bounded rework. Unresolvable uncertainty is surfaced, not hidden.

## V0 exclusions
Do not include by default:
- autonomous email/LinkedIn/DM sending;
- scraping that violates access restrictions or applicable terms;
- purchased lead databases without Owner-approved commercial/legal terms;
- sensitive personal data;
- unverifiable personal contact details;
- guarantees of sales, meetings, or revenue;
- impersonation of the customer;
- regulated/high-risk customer categories without a deliberate later policy.

## Upsell hypothesis after evidence
If customers value the research pack, test a recurring managed-acquisition offer that adds approved outreach, reply triage, optimization, and CRM handoff.

Do not build recurring sending infrastructure before V0 sale/delivery evidence exists.

## V0 business gates
### First-dollar gate
At least one unrelated stranger voluntarily purchases and receives the deliverable.

### Initial validation gate
At least 5 paid jobs across at least 3 unrelated customers with positive aggregate direct contribution.

### Repeatability gate
At least 10 paid jobs with evidence of acceptable quality, controlled rework/refunds, improving fulfillment efficiency, and at least some repeat/upsell/referral demand.

If these repeatedly fail after disciplined acquisition and offer experiments, change or kill the wedge rather than adding more agents.
